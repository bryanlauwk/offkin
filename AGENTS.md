# Project architecture

- OFFKIN’s public experience is a conversation-first brand-world co-creation journey for business/marketing buyers, with personal collectibles as a secondary use. The current public headline is “Your brand. Made collectible.” Pair one concrete gifting, launch and display benefit with the brand-name/website entry.
- Read public logo, logo-link, and site-title values from `site_settings`; existing admin controls remain authoritative. Preserve later server-side admin-auth fixes.
- The original Airbnb, A24 and Tesla concept boards in `public/canvas-worlds` set the visual richness standard. Their dense connected worlds, brand marks, colour, characters and architecture must not collapse into a neutral product catalogue or a generic object with props. These studies are unofficial, unvalidated references, not client work or available products.
- The public conversation and complete result board require the exact `offkin-proposal-v10` capability, including reference-image support. One explicit Generate action creates world, image-conditioned physical hero, components/interaction sheet and packaging. A conversational Update action plans and generates only its dependency closure. Never downgrade to a text-only or older contract. Preserve the v9 two-image canvas at `?canvas=legacy` and old `#world=` shares, plus v8 concept links through `LegacyConcept`.
- The entry is one brand-name or website input leading to bounded online discovery, then an automatic collectible-led preview. Ask a short follow-up only when evidence or identity is ambiguous or insufficient. Keep a factual manual-story fallback. Advanced fields and element choices stay under Edit details. Generated results use full-width editorial boards and one refinement composer. This is a guided conversation, not a live general-purpose AI chat. Generated images use structured element cards; do not claim arbitrary raster segmentation, direct pixel editing or live multi-user collaboration. Selections, hero, replacements and notes must flow into the stored brief and explicit physical generation.
- The three reference boards are output-quality standards, not homepage content or customer results. New sessions have no example-derived IDs, story, selected elements or hero. The generated board shows only its own actual four images with verified world/physical lineage. Never invent extra views, exploded diagrams or component crops. Legacy example previews remain separate from customer proposals.
- Drafts are local by default. Sharing requires a data review, defaults to a text-only bounded snapshot without generated IDs, and creates a separate editable branch. Image access is a separate opt-in requiring review of the original stored context/design metadata and an anyone-with-link warning. Image UUIDs retain the existing private capability-link behavior. Never broaden bucket, table or account permissions for this flow.
- New proposals are preview-first: brand/IP → rich concept preview → refine → request a quotation and realistic build proposal → engineering/prototype/production. Match the reference richness with tangible object appearance, layered silhouettes, strong narrative and playful proposed interaction. Do not dilute the preview through assembly templates, part-count ceilings or speculative manufacturing constraints. New generation requires the creative-preview capability and concept-preview-v1 metadata; legacy ProductPlan/compiler validation remains isolated for stored data and engineering tests. Assess actual parts, tolerances, mechanisms and cost in the later build proposal. Never claim generated views are validated geometry, working mechanisms or production evidence. No purchase or invented submission destination.
- Before release, run tests, type checks, build and mocked generation contracts, then inspect desktop/mobile layouts and perform bounded authorized live world → physical generation, refinement, restore and share checks. Do not claim public readiness from a GET health check alone.

- Proposal assets save independently. Persist partial progress, resume missing stages, preserve the accepted complete version during revisions, and switch atomically when its full dependency closure is ready. Stop, double-click, navigation and restore must not trigger stale generation or overwrite newer state. Completed cache results can be restored, but concurrent/unresolved provider requests do not provide exactly-once billing.
- Proposal image edits use bytes downloaded from validated saved private storage rows, never client-provided image URLs. The v10 capability is separately gated until the configured gateway reference-image route is verified; never silently fall back to prompt-only images.
- The public home keeps its simple editorial entry, while completed concepts expose refinement and a “Request a Quote & Build Proposal” handoff. The handoff prepares a local downloadable draft and an explicitly reviewed WhatsApp click-to-chat message to the owner-selected +60 14-930 3546. Opening WhatsApp never implies sending or receipt; images/files must be attached by the buyer. Keep public generation paused. Private generation requires the exact server-validated offkin-pilot-v1 invitation contract, approved protected setup and a separately authorized deployment.
- Example worlds live on `/showcase` in the existing responsive editorial layout. Original logo-free concept artwork in `src/assets/showcase/` targets the references' rich silhouettes and detail. Full concept boards show proposed views, details and packaging with explicit AI/unverified labels and accessible zoom; do not represent their geometric drift as CAD consistency. Use plain-text brand identification and unofficial caveats. Tesla stories must not imply SpaceX/Mars is Tesla business.

- Do not force repeated campaign slogans into the customer journey. Use concise service explanations beside relevant visuals. Use the note: “Concept preview. Final design, functionality and pricing confirmed during the build proposal.”
- World illustration language may draw on fine black outlines, warm ivory, restrained connecting accent routes, dense navigable isometric collage, playful scale shifts and tiny everyday scenes. Translate into premium stylized collectible forms, not realistic miniature property developments. Do not copy the supplied book composition/characters or impose yellow/Japan on all brands.

- Buyer-pilot enquiry fields are separate local browser drafts, never implicit public-share content. Disclose browser/shared-device persistence, support clear and preserve unsaved in-memory work after storage failure. Keep contact optional. Only an explicit WhatsApp review includes a bounded commercial summary; exclude image URLs, UUIDs, invite credentials, detailed story/notes and contact fields from its URL.
- Invite credentials are memory-only and travel in a dedicated HTTPS header; strip imported invite fragments from navigation immediately. Do not persist credentials in proposal sessions, localStorage, exports, share URLs or logs. Anonymous/legacy paid paths must fail closed. Keep restore as the existing explicit capability-link behavior. New source/cache reuse must be owned by the authenticated invite.
