# Project architecture

- Keep the public site as one commercial brand-world and co-creation journey. The October 2026 direction uses authored concept worlds, editable brief choices, and a clear path toward a prototype; do not reintroduce a conventional product catalogue or fake checkout.
- Read public logo, logo-link, and site-title values from `site_settings` so existing admin controls remain the source of truth.
- Concept studies are unofficial, unvalidated references, never client work or available products. Scale follows the story, experience and cost rather than a palm-size restriction.
- New co-creation generation requires the exact v8 capability contract. Never downgrade or truncate a rich brief for an older live backend. Brief download, local resume and reviewed sharing must remain usable without generation.
