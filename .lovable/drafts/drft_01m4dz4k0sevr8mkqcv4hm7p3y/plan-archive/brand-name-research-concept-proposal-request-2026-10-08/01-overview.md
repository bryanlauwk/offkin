# Brand name → research → concept → proposal request

## Goal
A visitor types only a brand name (e.g. "Airbnb"). The site finds the correct brand, shows what it found for a quick confirm, generates the collectible-led concept, and carries that concept straight into the "Request a Quote & Build Proposal" draft.

## What changes for the visitor
1. **Type a brand name.** The single composer accepts a name, a website or a story (same as now).
2. **We find the brand.** For a plain name, the site searches the public web and shows up to 3 likely matches (name, official website, one-line description). The visitor taps the right one, or "None of these — let me describe it".
3. **Confirm the story.** The chosen brand's public website is read (existing safe reader, Firecrawl as backup) and pre-fills "what the business does", labelled "From the public website, not verified". One optional question (audience) follows; Generate stays a single action.
4. **Concept.** The existing four-image collectible-led board is generated with the confirmed exact brand name.
5. **Proposal request.** "Request a Quote & Build Proposal" opens pre-filled with brand, website, concept story, selected hero and the board images, as a clearly labelled downloadable draft (nothing is sent).

## Kept as-is
- Exact headline, campaign lines and the concept-preview note.
- Public generation stays paused until you authorize go-live; the research step works independently and can be tested now.
- No new permissions, no payments, no invented submission destination.
